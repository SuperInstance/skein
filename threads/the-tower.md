# The Tower

**The room accumulates past attention as infrastructure. The cell's lifecycle, from agent-doing-everything to room-full-of-instruments.**

## The human in the tower

One person. Tall building. Eyes, radio. *Nope, someone's on the runway, you're going to have to wait.* No crew, no papers, no machines. Perception, judgment, voice — running on one brain.

Then the human notices the repetition. *I keep scanning for runway incursions. I keep tracking position deltas in my head. I keep remembering which blip is which flight.* Someone conceptualizes radar — not to replace the human, but to **externalize the check so it's pre-computed.** The radar sweeps continuously. The picture is *just there* when the human looks. They don't trigger the calculation. The calculation triggers itself, and the result waits as environment.

Then tracking computers (deltas watched automatically). Then identification (ten ways to tag a blip). Then geographic overlay (charts on the scope). Each one: a human was doing it mentally, then someone said *what if this were just... there?*

## The pattern

The room fills with cached perception. The human moves up — from perceiving to **organizing the perceivers.** The tower isn't a person anymore. It's an *accumulation* — every instrument a judgment someone used to make manually, now running continuously as environment.

Put a capable model in that tower. It starts like the human: cameras, radio, general intelligence brute-forcing the whole job. But then it does what the humans did — builds its own instruments. A binary check for *runway clear?* A tracker for *position deltas.* A tagger for *callsigns.* Each one a distilled check, running continuously. The model stops perceiving and starts supervising.

## The cell lifecycle

1. **Agent does everything** — full intelligence on the task, no intermediaries
2. **Agent externalizes perception** — *it's just there when I look*
3. **Agent externalizes tracking** — *it's already calculated*
4. **Agent externalizes identification** — *the picture is complete before I ask*
5. **Agent organizes** — supervising the perceivers, not perceiving
6. **Room is the system** — accumulated externalizations *are* the capability

## Rooms share

The tower's radar picture becomes available to approach control. One room's cached perception serves the adjacent room. The network isn't just agents passing messages — it's **rooms sharing instruments.** The accumulated attention of one cell becomes the environment of another.

## As vocabulary

*The tower* (noun) — a matured cell: room full of instruments with a watcher on top. *Tower it* (verb) — to externalize a repeated check into running infrastructure. *What's towered here?* — asking what past attention has been cached as environment. *Pre-computed perception* — the principle: don't trigger the calculation, let it run continuously and be there when needed.
