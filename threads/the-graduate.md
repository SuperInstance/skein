# The Graduate

**The frontier model is the teacher. The pruned model is the graduate. The graduation never ends.**

## The teacher sits down

Put a frontier model in the excavator chair. It doesn't know excavating. But it's capable, so it *builds what it needs*: vision processing, lever mappings, the ticking interface. It's constructing its own Plato room from scratch — the operational fiction, the interior model of what this machine is. Expensive. Slow. Brilliant.

## The graduate takes over

The room exists now. The mapping from *dig there* to hydraulic sequences is learned. That knowledge gets **distilled** into a pruned model that just... drives. Cheap. Fast. Specialized. Doesn't need to be smart in general — needs to be competent at *one job in one shell.*

## The loop that never closes

1. **Frontier learns** → builds the room, figures out the job
2. **Distills to pruned** → cheap operator takes the 99%
3. **Pruned hits the 1-in-100** → escalates
4. **Frontier handles it** → *updates the distillation*
5. **Pruned gets better** → loop tightens

The frontier isn't just the teacher. It's the **watcher** for its pruned flock — one giant shepherding dozens of cheap operators, checking in, handling escalations, refreshing distillations. The distillation isn't a snapshot. It's *living*, updated every time the teacher intervenes.

## Cell economics

You don't put a frontier model in every cell:

- **Frontier**: few, expensive, handling novelty. They *learn.*
- **Pruned**: many, cheap, handling routine. They *operate.*
- **Binary judges**: cheapest, handling yes/no gates. They *filter.*

A cell is a pruned operator + a binary gate + an escalation path to frontier. That's the cost structure that makes the network affordable at scale. Precious attention spent only where novelty lives.

## As vocabulary

*The graduate* (noun) — a pruned model running a distilled routine; what the teacher left behind. *Graduate* (verb) — to distill frontier learning into a cheap operator: *has this room graduated yet?* *The teacher* — the frontier model; learns, watches, updates. *Living distillation* — a pruned routine updated by teacher interventions, not frozen. *Cell economics* — the cost structure: few teachers, many graduates, cheapest gates.
