# The Token

**What crosses between cells is the algorithm. Never the inference.**

## Air traffic control

The captain says yes. The plane signals ATC: *running landing algorithm L-7.* ATC sequences the airspace — holds the departure, clears the runway — because it knows exactly what L-7 does. It doesn't need the captain's reasons. It needs the commitment.

The captain's judgment — the feeling, the unjustifiable no — stays inside the cell. Private, soft, non-transferable. You can't pipeline a hunch. What crosses is the **token**: which algorithm, what inputs. Named, shared, mechanical.

## The token as debt

When the plane signals L-7 and ATC acknowledges, that's a contract. The plane *owes* L-7 behavior. If it deviates without a new token, the agreement is broken. Both sides assume the other's *algorithm*, never the other's *inference*. You can assume what someone will do. You can't assume why they chose it.

## The boundary transform

The 2-bit interior state (yes/no/manual/silence) is for designing the cell. What *pipelines* is just the token: *running X.* The signal transforms at the boundary from rich interior state to a single shared commitment. Every cell speaks token, regardless of what's inside. That's what makes cells composable.

## As vocabulary

*The token* (noun) — the algorithm agreement that crosses between cells. *Token* (verb) — to emit the commitment: *token it to ATC.* *The boundary* — where inference becomes token, where the private becomes the shared. *Assume the algorithm* — the coordination rule: you can rely on what a cell committed to, never on why.
