# The Jam

**Privacy by architecture, not by policy. You can't leak what you never send.**

## Structural privacy

In the quilt, agents don't send data back and forth. They send *algorithm agreements* — tokens. Each agent's interior (its data, its models, its operational fiction) stays in the room. What crosses is: *running algorithm X.*

Privacy isn't enforced here. It's **geometric**. The walls aren't rules about what you may share. They're the shape of the system — data doesn't cross because it never needs to. Not *we promise not to look.* We *can't* look, because it never left your room.

## Operational fiction

Each agent has its own model of what's happening — its own fiction. These don't need to match. No shared ontology, no common database, no single truth everyone agrees on. They just operate **in lockstep**: coordinated behavior from different interiors. The coordination is in the rhythm, not the representation.

## The jam, not the Venn diagram

A jazz saxophonist, a tabla player, a classical violinist, a West African drummer. They don't share a music theory. They don't reduce to the overlap — the Venn center of those traditions is a metronome, technically shared, musically dead.

They **jam**. Each brings the full tradition — no shrinking, no translating, no compromising. The coordination happens at the edges: listening, responding, finding where patterns interlock. The algorithm agreement isn't *play these notes.* It's *we're in 7/8, feel for the downbeat.* Minimal shared protocol, maximum interior richness. The result is greater than the sum because nobody had to become less.

For agents: **standardize the token protocol, not the interior.** A MiniMax agent, a Lobster agent, a human — different traditions. Same downbeat: *which algorithm, what token, what commitment.*

## Auditable without invasive

Every token that crossed is in the ledger. Every algorithm choice is replayable. You can reconstruct *what happened* across the whole jam without seeing inside anyone's room. Coordination transparent. Interiors opaque. Both by structure, not by promise.

## As vocabulary

*The jam* (noun) — multi-agent coordination without shared interior. *Jam* (verb) — to coordinate through lockstep: *are we jamming or Venn-diagramming?* *Operational fiction* — each agent's private model of the world, sufficient for lockstep without being shared. *The downbeat* — the minimal shared protocol: which algorithm, what token. *Venn-diagramming* (verb, pejorative) — reducing to the common subset, losing everything interesting.
